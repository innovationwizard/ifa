The Holy Grail
As it should be clearly explained in the genesis docs, connect to bank is THE HOLY GRAIL. 
The reason why no app like this has ever existed in Guatemala is because no bank EVER has granted a rest api or any other kind of connection to... well, NOBODY. They are stubborn CLOSED to the idea. 
This app is not a novel idea at all. Any decent country has one or multiple apps like this one, and they have had them for decades. 
And: That's why I'm building the app NOW. A friend of mine, a former bank vp, is onboard with the idea and has verbally offered to do something in the spectrum of "testing the waters with one bank" to "pushing the envelope with a small group of banks" , depending on the traction that my app is able to gather. 
So: If he, by miracle, gets a green light, this app MUST BE READY to connect to any given guatemalan bank IN NO TIME, before they think it twice. 
Meanwhile, for the gaining traction period, the default flow is: (1) user downloads his bank statements from the bank website, in whichever format they come, and (2) user uploads to this app his bank statements in whichever format they were downloaded.
